Privacy Notice

Information about how we use personal data

Metaria ry respects the privacy of its members, applicants, artists, workers and people contacting the association. This notice explains what information we process, why, and how to exercise your rights.

1 Controller and contact

Metaria ry · Business ID 2761325-3 · Laitatuulenkaari 3 A 2, 00850 Helsinki, Finland · www.metaria.org. Privacy enquiries may be submitted using the contact details published on our website.

2 Information we collect

Membership applications may include name, email, telephone number, municipality, membership or Board-related interests, reasons for joining and a link to professional work. Our statutory member register includes members’ names and domiciles; organisational members may have named contact persons. For employment, fees and financial administration, we may process contracts, invoices, tax information, Finnish personal identity codes, bank details, payslips and related accounting documents where necessary. Website enquiries contain information you choose to submit. We do not request bank details for membership applications.

3 Why we use information

We process information to assess applications, administer membership and association governance, communicate about activities, respond to enquiries, arrange employment or services, pay remuneration and meet tax, accounting and other legal duties. Our legal bases, as applicable, are legal obligation (GDPR Article 6(1)(c)), contract (6(1)(b)), and legitimate interests in running the association (6(1)(f)). Publishing an optional member profile or professional link requires specific consent (6(1)(a)), which may be withdrawn.

4 Who can access information

Access is limited according to duties. Current authorised Board members handle membership and governance records; information relevant to membership decisions may be shared with the Board. Employment and financial information is restricted to authorised administrators and persons who need it for their duties. Where required, data may be provided to banks, payroll providers, auditors, insurers and public authorities.

5 Website, systems and service providers

Metaria uses Namecheap for website hosting, domain and Private Email services. Website contact-form messages are delivered to the association’s official email inbox, accessible to the Chair and Secretary/Treasurer, and stored in that inbox. Other services include Google Drive, Microsoft 365, Adobe, YouTube, Palkka.fi and locally installed accounting software. Providers process information only to the extent relevant to the services used; YouTube has its own privacy information for platform users.

6 International processing and security

Some service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where GDPR international-transfer rules apply, transfers must rely on an appropriate legal mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses with any necessary supplementary measures. Metaria uses role-based access restrictions and appropriate organisational and technical safeguards. The precise processing locations depend on the services and account arrangements.

7 How long we keep information

Membership information is kept while membership is active and thereafter only as justified by legal or administrative needs. Application records are reviewed when no longer required. Website enquiries are reviewed after resolution and normally deleted within 12 months, unless further retention is justified. Employment contracts and related records are retained as necessary for employment, legal claims and statutory obligations. Under Finnish accounting rules, accounting books and financial statements are generally retained for 10 years; supporting vouchers generally for 6 years, subject to applicable calculation rules and exceptions. Payroll accounting may require 10 years. Records are not kept indefinitely merely because they are electronic.

8 Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability where applicable, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact Metaria through its published website contact details. We normally respond within one month. You may also complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi). This notice is reviewed when Metaria’s processing arrangements materially change.
